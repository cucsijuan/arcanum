## What's new
- **More cards**: a sacrifice-themed commander deck now plays in full, with its sacrifice outlets, death triggers and cards that come back from the graveyard.
- **Extort**: whenever you cast a spell, each permanent with extort lets you pay {W/B} to drain each opponent for 1.
- **Crimes**: abilities that trigger whenever you commit a crime (targeting an opponent, something they control or a card in their graveyard).
- **Rules**: extra mana from Swamps, mana equal to your devotion to a color, sacrifices that must share a card type, and death triggers that trigger an additional time (also when the permanent that grants it dies at the same time).

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. This version needs the content module 0.23.0: install `arcanum-classic-v0.23.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…).
3. The first start after updating may take a moment to refresh the card data.
