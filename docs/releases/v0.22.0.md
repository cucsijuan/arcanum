## What's new
- **Career: buying and selling single cards** (with module 0.11.0, from October 13):
  - **The store's counter has a case of singles**: about 20 cards a day from the sets on the shelf at steady prices (a markup by rarity), now and then a foil or a special art (borderless, showcase, extended art…), which cost at least three times the usual card. Selling to the store moved to the counter too.
  - **Buy & Sell on the forum**: 10 to 15 players list cards every day at prices that swing either way, sometimes a bargain, sometimes steep. Buy at their price or make an offer: they take it, counter or turn it down.
  - **Sell your cards on the forum**: post a card at your price. Over the next days it may sell, get a lower offer you can take or decline, or nothing. The fairer the price, the sooner it sells; above what anyone would pay, it never does. After a week it comes down and the card is back.
  - Buying and selling straight back to the store never makes money.
- The website's **Sell cards** page is gone: sell at the store's counter or on the forum.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Content module: `arcanum-classic-v0.11.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…) turns the trading on. The first start after installing it downloads the card data again (a few minutes).
