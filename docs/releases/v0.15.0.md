## What's new
- **Magic Origins**: the whole set plays by its rules, with its boosters for draft and sealed (and their pack picture when you open them) and five two-color sample decks. It needs the content module 0.7.0.
- **New rules for it**: renown and "becomes renowned" triggers, block taxes ("creatures can't block unless their controller pays"), "can block only creatures with flying", tapping artifacts as a cost, prevention shields on one creature, damage and life gain replacements (doubling life gain, turning an opponent's life gain into loss, "+1 damage from your red sources"), "exile it instead of dying", casting spells from among revealed cards while a spell resolves, naming a card to exile it from every zone, storage counters, sweeping hands, graveyards and permanents back into libraries, emblems for other players, and more.
- **Rules fixes**: an ability's "this" is only the object it came from, so a creature that left and came back is a new object; counters, power and damage of an object that died are those it had as it died; tokens that died can't come back; creatures reanimated "with counters" enter with them; a card that lost its abilities also loses its taxes, evasion and replacement effects; "up to N targets" works for spells and activated abilities; two Alhammarret's Archives make a draw four draws; "Sacrifice a creature" costs can sacrifice the creature itself; colorless artifact creature tokens (Thopters, Servos, Myr…) are artifacts.
- **Booster pictures**: a booster's picture shows as soon as the pack appears (it used to wait behind the pack's card pictures the first time).

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Get the content module: download `arcanum-classic-v0.7.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest and, in Arcanum, open **Extras → Install module from zip…**. This version of the game needs module 0.7.0 or later, and module 0.7.0 needs this version of the game.
3. The first start after installing the new module downloads the card data again (a few minutes).
