## What's new
- **Career: a new look for your room**, with a door that takes you to the store and a bigger box with your cards.
- **The store has a second table**: career matches are played at one or the other, and each day's events at that day's table.
- **Deck rules**: a card that says "A deck can have up to nine cards named …" (or another number) allows that many copies in Commander too, as the rules say.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Content module: `arcanum-classic-v0.11.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…), if you don't have it yet.
