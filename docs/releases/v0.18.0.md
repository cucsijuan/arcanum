## What's new
- **Career mode**: the life of a card player, on its own pixel-art screens. Your room is home: its computer opens the store's website, the boxes on the shelf hold your decks, and the store sells boosters and preconstructed decks and buys your cards.
  - Start with a preconstructed deck, some cash and boosters; play two free **daily matches** a day against the store's regulars, paid in cash or a booster.
  - **Practice** against the regulars you've met, and **ante matches** arranged in a chat: choose one of the cards they put up, offer one of yours, and the winner keeps both.
  - **Weekly challenges**, the day's **offers**, and **rivals** who improve their decks as the weeks go by.
  - **Events** on the store's calendar: draft night, commander night with pods of four, the Saturday tournament and the monthly championship, entered with cash, boosters or qualification tokens.
  - **Deck building on a desk**: your cards and the deck laid out on a playmat, with search and filters.
  - **Prices that move**: the decks that win the store's weekly tournaments put their cards in demand, and the week's featured set sells a little lower.
  - **Every kind of booster** a set was sold in, with **foils** and **surge foils** only where those printings really exist.
- **Sign in with Discord** (Settings, Online, or when you open Online): your online identity follows your Discord account to any computer, and your Discord name can become your player name. Playing on the internet now needs it; the local network doesn't.
- **Your career in the cloud**: signed in with Discord, your career is saved online after each change and comes down on another computer. If you played on two computers without syncing, you choose which career to keep (the other is kept as a backup file).
- **The store's season changes without updates**: special events, featured sets, booster prices and news come from the game's service; when you're online, the career uses the service's clock.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Get the content module: download `arcanum-classic-v0.8.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest and, in Arcanum, open **Extras → Install module from zip…**. This version of the game needs module 0.8.0 or later (the card data downloads again the first time).
