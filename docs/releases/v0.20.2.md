## What's new
- **Ward warning**: targeting an opponent's permanent with ward now asks first and shows what ward will make you pay. Choose another target, cancel the spell or ability, or target it anyway.
- **Combat damage counts down**: a player's life counter shows the life before combat damage and drops as each attacker's blow lands.
- **Career: ante and practice with an unfinished deck**: the deck picker now starts on a deck you can play, and when the chosen deck can't be played the ante section says so and shows its buttons disabled (before, they just didn't appear).

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Content module: `arcanum-classic-v0.10.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…), if you don't have it yet.
