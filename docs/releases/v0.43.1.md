## What's new
- **Compact board, choosing among cards**: on the compact board (the one for consoles and small screens), a choice among cards uses the whole screen, in rows sized to fit, with the large preview at the left and the answers under. The d-pad reaches every card. "See the board" hides the cards and "Show the cards" brings them back.
- **Compact board, action panel**: the panel stays on screen with a thin frame, bigger text and a little more room inside. It shows the top card of the stack large beside it.
- **Controller**: using the d-pad hides the stick's pointer, and confirm presses the panel's focused answer at once.
- **Career with a controller**: the d-pad moves inside the computer and the store's notes. Back closes them, lists follow the focus, and the website and the boosters note fit a 1280-wide screen.
- **One log button**: the board has one log button, at the top right under undo, with a badge of unread entries. It replaces the two buttons.
- **Fixed**: a graveyard or exile opened to choose from no longer stays open after the choice.
- **Fixed**: closing the game while it was making cards or loading pictures could crash. On consoles, closing the game no longer ends in an error, and a crash when moving around career screens is fixed.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. This version needs the content module 0.28.0. The game offers it when it starts. You can also install `arcanum-classic-v0.28.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…).
3. The first start after updating may take a moment to refresh the card data.
