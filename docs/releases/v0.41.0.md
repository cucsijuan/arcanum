## What's new
- **Two new Standard sets**: a space-themed set and a turtle-themed set, each with its commander set. Every card is playable. Play and collector boosters are as released, including the bonus sheet. Sample decks, commander precons, draft ratings, career store and commander night entries are included.
- **Modal double-faced cards**: a card with two faces can be cast or played from hand, graveyard, exile or the command zone, choosing the face. A back-face land enters the battlefield as that face. Its mana value comes from the face being cast.
- **Fuse and sneak**: split cards with fuse can be cast with both halves from hand. Sneak costs are supported, with the triggers that check them.
- **More card vocabulary**: controlling another player during their next turn, additional upkeep steps, switching power and toughness, static land types, one noncreature spell per turn, modular, and copies entering from graveyards.
- **Commanders**: a legendary Vehicle, or a legendary Spacecraft with power and toughness, can be a commander.
- **"While" conditions**: static abilities that apply "as long as" one number is less than another now work.
- **Handheld and controller play**:
  - Narrow screens: the deck builders, the career deck desk, the career collection and the settings fit small screens. Less used actions move to a menu, filters sit on two rows, and descriptions get a strip under the options.
  - Touch: touching a card shows it first. Touching it again adds, takes out or picks it. Touching anything else puts the preview away.
  - Controller: the arrows reach the cards of the deck builders and the career collection. Confirm adds or picks the focused card, and the left face button takes it out. The back button presses a screen's Back button.
  - Handheld console play: one table at a time with the other players in a strip, touch and controller input, and faster card pictures.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. This version needs the content module 0.26.0: install `arcanum-classic-v0.26.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…).
3. The first start after updating may take a moment to refresh the card data.
