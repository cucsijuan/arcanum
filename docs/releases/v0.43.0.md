## What's new
- **Career tournaments play their real lists**: the competitive constructed decks some events bring now play their tournament lists exactly, sideboards included. No stand-in cards.
- **Many new card mechanics**: plot, bargain, collect evidence, earthbend, airbend, mayhem, teamwork, power-up and descend. New card rules too: abilities that work from exile, delayed abilities at the next combat, "can't cast spells from anywhere other than their hands", creatures that can't block your creatures, and more ways copies enter.
- **Exiled cards on the board**: the board offers the abilities of exiled cards that can be activated from exile.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. This version needs the content module 0.28.0. The game offers it when it starts. You can also install `arcanum-classic-v0.28.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…).
3. The first start after updating may take a moment to refresh the card data.
