## What's new
- **Magic 2010**: the whole set plays by its rules, with its boosters for draft and sealed and five two-color sample decks. It needs the content module 0.4.0.
- **Combat**: creatures that all able blockers must block, creatures that can block any number of attackers (dividing their damage), creatures that can't attack or block alone, "attacks you this turn if able", protection from creature types, and attack restrictions about the defending player. Attacks and blocks always obey as many requirements as the rules allow, also in big multiplayer boards.
- **Damage**: every damage event is one simultaneous event; prevention, redirection and doubling effects apply once each, and when their order matters the affected player chooses it. Fog-style prevention, redirection shields, damage divided as a spell is cast, random discards and many more effects are new.
- **Copies, libraries and hidden information**: entering as a copy, copying spells for each other player, shuffling permanents into libraries, revealed hands and revealed library tops, choosing any card name, and Auras that enter without being cast choose what they enchant.
- **Fixes**: a player can't win while an opponent can't lose, a draw from an empty library is only counted at the next check, and spells or abilities whose targets must be different need enough different targets.

## Install
1. Download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Get the content module: download `arcanum-classic-v0.4.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest and, in Arcanum, open **Extras → Install module from zip…**. This version of the game needs module 0.4.0 or later, and module 0.4.0 needs this version of the game.
3. The first start after installing or updating the module downloads the card data (a few minutes).
