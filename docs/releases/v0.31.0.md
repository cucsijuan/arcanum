## What's new
- **Another Commander product**: its four preconstructed Commander decks as released (official lists, foil commanders), on the career store's shelf and at commander night.
- **Previous Standard set**: its Collector Boosters with the published odds, and its Starter Kit on the career store's shelf.
- **New rules**: escape (cast from your graveyard by exiling other cards; some enter with counters), ravenous, toxic and vanishing read from the card text, fear, Auras that enchant a player (shown on that player's side, with "Enchanting …" on the card's status), abilities activated from the command zone, offspring gained from another permanent, costs where you sacrifice or tap X permanents, tempting offers, parley, extra-turn gifts, token creation replacements (one of each, extra tokens, a different creature instead), "can't draw more than one card each turn", granted cascade, every creature type, attacks repeated in an additional combat, and many more card abilities.
- **Fixes**:
  - Mana granted by a static ability keeps its spending restriction.
  - Hideaway puts the other cards on the bottom in a random order.
  - "Enters with counters" from spells and other effects are on the permanent as it enters.
  - Permanents phasing out together with their Auras or Equipment phase back in once.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Content module: `arcanum-classic-v0.19.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…), for the new set. This version of the game is needed for it.
3. The first start after updating downloads the card data again (a few minutes).
