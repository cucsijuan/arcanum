## What's new
- **More cards**: another Standard set and its commander set are fully playable, with their boosters, sample decks and four commander decks in career mode.
- **Rooms**: cast either door of a Room, then unlock the other door later at sorcery speed by paying its cost; the board announces each door that is unlocked or locked.
- **Manifest dread**: look at the top two cards of your library, put one onto the battlefield face down as a 2/2 and the other into your graveyard.
- **New keywords and abilities**: eerie, survival, megamorph, retrace and coven, plus many new card-wide rules, votes, curses and clones.
- **Rules**: a planeswalker that is a creature everywhere except on the battlefield can be your commander; when a spell's extra cost taps creatures or lands, paying its mana keeps enough of them untapped.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. This version needs the content module 0.24.0: install `arcanum-classic-v0.24.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…).
3. The first start after updating may take a moment to refresh the card data.
