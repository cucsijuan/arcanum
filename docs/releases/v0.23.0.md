## What's new
- **Playmats in the career**: a career starts without a playmat; the store's shelf above the computer sells plain playmats in the built-in colors ($15) and the nebula ($18). Your playmat lies on the table at your matches and events, and you can swap between the ones you own. Regulars and visitors bring their own.
- **Career tables**: each player's rows of cards are laid out on their playmat, the divider between the two sides sits a little higher so your three rows fit on the table, and the second table is as deep as the first.
- **Commander suggestions**: the deck builder suggests cards for a commander from community statistics (most played and highest synergy cards by group, themes and budget, the average deck's card types beside yours, and the average deck itself). On the career desk the suggestions show which cards you own, and the average deck is built from your collection.
- **Draft statistics**: computer drafters pick using the set's draft statistics, by skill (career regulars by reputation tier, visitors lower), and always build playable decks; career players see grades on the draft table and the deck desk.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Content module: `arcanum-classic-v0.12.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…), for the draft statistics and the commander suggestions.
