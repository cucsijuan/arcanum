## What's new
- **Another Standard set and its commander set**: every card plays by its rules, with play and collector boosters as released for draft and sealed, five two-color sample decks, the two commander precons in the career store and at commander night, and draft ratings for the computer drafters. It needs the content module 0.21.0.
- **New rules**: blight, wither, evoke (also when an effect gives it), encore, clash, manifest, companion, champion, persist and undying, behold costs, mana that can't pay generic costs, abilities only your opponents may activate, and many more effects for the new cards.
- **Board**: a sacrificed permanent, a countered spell or ability, revealed cards, a choice made and a change of control are now announced on the board.
- **Fixes**:
  - "Whenever one or more creatures you control deal combat damage to a player" triggers once for each player dealt damage.
  - Reversible cards (the same card on both sides) keep their printings.
  - The computer stops a loop that only goes on while it keeps saying yes to an optional action.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Update the content module too: `arcanum-classic-v0.21.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…).
3. The first start after updating downloads the card data again (a few minutes), to import the printings that were missing.
