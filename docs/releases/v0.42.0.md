## What's new
- **Computer opponents think ahead**: the computer plans around its own deck. It guesses the opponent's hidden cards only from what could be in their card pool, never from their deck. It tries its options in copies of the game played out a few turns ahead, for its main-phase actions, attacks, blocks and instants. Computer seats in hosted games think ahead too. The new "Computer thinks ahead" setting turns this off. Devices with fewer than six processors use a smaller search with a longer time budget.
- **Career opponents play real-world decks**: the regulars and the event visitors bring decks built from real play data, one for each archetype of each set, with cards that fit the archetype's plan. Some events also bring competitive constructed decks taken from top-finishing tournament lists. The starting choice and the store's 60-card decks are the official starter decks. The old generated sample decks are gone.
- **Console**: the console build updates itself from the release, and Restart works there. The update notice says when the download is installed and shows the release page's address. A question shown over the screen takes the controller's focus at once, so one press answers it.
- **Career screens**: the back tag says Exit, Back home or Close, and a place's name no longer sits under the screen's own tags.
- **Fixes**:
  - A trigger whose targets can't all be chosen once X is known is removed.
  - A spell or ability with no legal targets can't be cast or activated.
  - The update check tries again while the network comes up.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. This version needs the content module 0.27.0. The game offers it when it starts. You can also install `arcanum-classic-v0.27.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…).
3. The first start after updating may take a moment to refresh the card data.
