## What's new
- **Resolve all this turn**: when something is on the stack, a new button next to "Resolve" lets every spell and ability resolve without asking you to respond until the turn ends. Your marked phase stops still stop you. Works online too.
- **Can't-be-blocked mark**: creatures that can't be blocked show a ⊘ in their status corner. This covers creatures that can't be blocked when printed, from an effect, or only by a particular player's creatures this turn. The card's status panel says by whom when it's only one player's creatures.
- **Computer players and sacrifice costs**: they no longer sacrifice their permanents, discard or return cards just to draw a card or make a token. They sacrifice their least valuable things (tokens, small artifacts) to draw when they have no spells left in hand. They never pick their commander to sacrifice when there's another choice.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Content module: `arcanum-classic-v0.11.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…), if you don't have it yet.
