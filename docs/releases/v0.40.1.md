## What's new
- **Prevention order**: when several prevention effects could stop the same damage and the order changes something (which shield or counter gets used up, or whether a "when damage is prevented" effect happens), the player being dealt the damage now chooses which applies first. Computer players keep their resources when they can.
- **Continuous effects that depend on each other**: inside each layer, an effect that would change whether another exists, what it applies to or what it does now applies first, whatever their timestamps. Example: "all creatures lose all abilities" now stops a creature's ability that grants something to other permanents, even if that creature came later.
- **Effects keep their objects**: an effect that starts to apply to some permanents keeps applying to them in later steps, even if its ability is lost on the way. Example: "noncreature artifacts are 2/2 creatures" now does make them 2/2.
- **Power and toughness checks**: effects that look at power or toughness ("creatures with power 2 or less …") now use the right values instead of ones left over from earlier.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. This version needs the content module 0.25.0: install `arcanum-classic-v0.25.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…).
3. The first start after updating may take a moment to refresh the card data.
