## What's new
- **Another Standard set**: every card playable, with its Play Boosters as released (seasonal full-art lands, special guest cards, showcase and borderless printings), two-color sample decks, draft ratings for the computer drafters and career draft grades, and a place on the career store's shelf and in the featured sets.
- **Offspring**: pay the optional offspring cost as you cast the creature, and a 1/1 token copy of it is created when it enters, even if it has left the battlefield in the meantime.
- **Forage**: exile three cards from your graveyard or sacrifice a Food, your choice, as a cost or as an optional effect. "Whenever you forage" abilities trigger on it.
- **Class enchantments**: they enter at level 1 and level up for a cost at sorcery speed, one level at a time. Each level adds its abilities. The board announces the new level and shows it on the card's status. Bots level up their Classes.
- **Expend**: abilities that trigger as you spend your fourth (or eighth) total mana on spells in a turn.
- **Pawprint modes**: spells that let you choose up to five pawprints' worth of modes, and the same mode may be chosen more than once.
- **More rules for the set**: abilities that trigger the first time each turn a creature becomes the target of your spells or abilities, gifts of tapped tokens, spells whose targets change when the gift is promised, "whenever you give a gift", cost reductions with colored mana, casting from your graveyard with an extra cost (life or foraging), tapping creatures to pay for an ability, sacrificing any number of permanents to reduce a spell's cost, doubling the counters you put on anything, and many more card abilities.
- **Choosing X can be cancelled**: if you start casting a spell or activating an ability with X by mistake, Cancel backs out without paying anything.
- **Targeting a stack of tokens**: a window opens with each token, so you can pick which one to target.
- **Season check**: an event with Swiss rounds by attendance is valid.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Content module: `arcanum-classic-v0.18.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…), for the new set. This version of the game is needed for it.
3. The first start after updating downloads the card data again (a few minutes).
