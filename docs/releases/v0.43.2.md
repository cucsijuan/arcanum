## What's new
- **Controller, opened packs**: the d-pad moves over the cards of an opened pack, with their large preview. It used to move over what was behind them. Confirm opens the pack, back skips the opening, and back on the cards closes them.
- **Controller, drafts**: the d-pad reaches the cards of a pick and the list of picks, in the career and outside it.
- **Controller, dialogs**: a question or the update dialog keeps the buttons to itself while it is open.
- **Draft and opened packs**: the cards are sized so the whole pack fits on the screen, and the list of picks stays on it too. On small screens the bottom row and the list's frame went off the bottom.
- **Fixed**: the store's website in the career could hang or close the game at some screen sizes.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. This version needs the content module 0.28.0. The game offers it when it starts. You can also install `arcanum-classic-v0.28.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…).
3. The first start after updating may take a moment to refresh the card data.
