## What's new
- **Four new Commander decks**: the preconstructed Commander decks of the latest set, with every card playable, and their Collector's Editions (the same lists, every card a surge foil). The career store sells them (45.00, Collector's Editions 95.00) and they come to commander night.
- **New rules for them**: intimidate, melee, evolve, training, convoke, unearth, rebound, blitz and foretell; support, adapt, monstrosity, explore and connive; shield counters; tiered spells; becoming a copy of a creature for good; playing cards exiled face down; casting a card from the graveyard as its Adventure; exiling a creature and bringing it back attacking at the next combat, unblockable; and many more card abilities.
- **Collector Boosters of the latest set**, as released: its special cards, the Commander decks' special printings and surge foils.
- **Career events in a format**: a constructed event can be played in a format (standard, modern…). Your deck must be legal in it, and the computer players bring legal decks too.
- **Monthly championship**: now a local event, cheaper ($25) and open to everyone, with smaller prizes and no qualification.
- **Your collection on the desk**: click the box of cards in your room to look through every card you own, with how many copies you have and what each goes for, how much of each set you have collected, and the set's scenes (cards whose art joins into one picture), the cards you're missing in grey. Right-click a card to sell it on the forum. The website's old "My cards" page is now "My log".
- **Copies everywhere you choose a card**: choosing a card to sell or to stake shows how many copies you have; "Hide cards in my decks" counts only the copies no deck uses.
- **Search by price and copies**: in the career, `usd>=2` (or `price:`, `p:`) finds cards by what they go for, `qty>=2` (or `copies:`, `owned:`) by the copies you have.
- **Readable tooltips** in the career (they were dark text on a dark background).
- **Bots and the board**: a bot pays X life only for a sweep that favors it, never all its life; life lost without damage is shown on the board; cards in graveyards or exile are chosen in the zone viewer, picking which pile to look in first. Forum sale posts are checked twice a day.
- **Rules fixes**: a creature put onto the battlefield attacking can attack any opponent or one of their planeswalkers, and the blockers and damage steps happen even when nothing else attacked. "Until the end of your next turn" lasts through that turn. A tiered spell is only offered when a mode with legal targets can be paid for.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Content module: `arcanum-classic-v0.15.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…), for the new Commander decks, Collector Boosters and scenes. This version of the game is needed for it.
