## What's new
- **The newest Standard set**: every card playable, with its Play Boosters and Collector Boosters as released (their special printings, foils and the odds of each slot), two-color sample decks, and a place on the career store's shelf and in the featured sets.
- **Its Commander deck**: the preconstructed deck with every card playable, as printed (its two foil commanders). The career store sells it (45.00) and it comes to commander night.
- **Fracture foils**: the set's fracture foil printings show their own finish, a holographic glitter of small triangular chips over silver, and are valued as such in the career. Other special foils show as regular foils.
- **Morph**: a card with morph can be cast face down as a 2/2 creature for {3} and turned face up for its morph cost any time you have priority. Only its controller sees what it is; it's revealed when it leaves the battlefield. The board shows it as a card back with its power and toughness, and announces when it's turned face up.
- **New rules for the set and its deck**: preparation cards (a prepared creature lets you cast a copy of its spell), empowering, impending, incubate, toxic, monocolored hybrid mana, protection from a card type for a player and their creatures, Map tokens, goading for the rest of the game, giving permanents to different opponents, "whenever you create one or more tokens", delayed abilities at the next end step, and many more card abilities.
- **Sideboards in games**: the cards of a deck's sideboard (in limited, the pool's unused cards) are outside the game, for cards that fetch or use them.
- **Bots**: a bot casts a card face down only when it can't pay its full cost and turns its creatures face up before combat, and chooses creature when asked for a card type to be protected from.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Content module: `arcanum-classic-v0.17.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…), for the new set and Commander deck. This version of the game is needed for it.
3. The first start after updating downloads the card data again (a few minutes).
