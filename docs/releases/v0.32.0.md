## What's new
- **Cardmarket prices and deck cost**:
  - Each printing stores its market prices (Cardmarket euros and TCGplayer dollars, foil and nonfoil).
  - The deck builder shows each card's Cardmarket price on its tile, and the section ends with the total price of the deck list.
  - Commander deck suggestions can be sorted by lowest or highest price.
  - Search cards by price with `eur<1`, `usd>=5`, etc., and sort search results with `order:eur` or `order:usd` (by the cheapest printing, cards with no price last).
- **Printing and land search**:
  - Search by printing style and frame features: `border:borderless`, `frame:showcase`, `frame:2015`, `is:fullart`, `is:etched`, `is:glossy`, `is:textless`, `is:firstprint`, `is:reserved`, and `is:gamechanger`.
  - Search land cycles by their common nicknames: `is:shockland`, `is:fetchland`, `is:triome`, `is:dual`, `is:bounceland`, `is:canopyland`, `is:painland`, `is:fastland`, `is:slowland`, `is:surveilland`, and more.
- **Double-faced cards preview**: hovering a double-faced card on any menu screen (deck builder, collection, limited draft, career event, career desk) displays its other face beside it.
- **Blocking token stacks**: picking an attacker in a stack of identical tokens to be blocked assigns the block to the first unblocked attacker and separates it from the stack, leaving the rest grouped to be blocked.
- **Bot decisions**:
  - Bots make smarter choices on additional costs and optional payments: they prefer paying mana rather than sacrificing permanents or discarding cards, protect their commander, and weigh optional sacrifices against what the payoff gives.
  - Bots avoid sacrificing permanents for top-of-library looks (scry or surveil) unless they have no castable spells left in hand.
  - Bots evaluate whether a counterspell is worth its casting cost, avoiding sacrifices that cost more than the spell being countered.
  - Bots use spells that reduce stats ("gets -N/-N") as combat tricks to win blocks, and cast shrink or burn spells outside combat only when they kill the target.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. The content module stays the same: `arcanum-classic-v0.19.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…) if you don't have it yet.
3. The first start after updating downloads the card data again (a few minutes), to import the market prices and printing details.
