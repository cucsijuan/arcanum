## What's new
- **Combat, blow by blow**: each creature dealing combat damage rises, lunges at what it damages (a creature, a planeswalker or a player's life), lands with a burst of light and the damage it deals, and goes back to its place, a moment passing before the next creature's blows, so you can follow what each card does.
- **Winning an event is celebrated**: when you win a draft or sealed event, the cards of your deck bounce across the screen, leaving trails behind them.
- **Your tokens' pictures**: the deck builder's new Tokens tab lists the tokens your deck's cards make, with every picture each token was printed with; choose the ones you like and each game shows one of them (with none chosen, any of them). The first start downloads the card data again to get all the token pictures.
- **Cards under cards**: a card exiled until a permanent leaves the battlefield lies under that permanent instead of in the exile pile, and says what exiled it. Auras, Equipment and those cards lie behind their permanent one under the other with every title showing, and pointing at any of them brings the whole group to the front.
- **Online**: your opponents see the playmat you chose for your side of the table (custom images stay on your device). The settings call the two playmats "Your playmat" and "Opponents' playmat".
- **Draft**: the pick clock is larger, with a bar emptying under the header, a soft call at ten seconds and quiet ticks at the end; another player's pick no longer clears the card you selected, and lists keep where you scrolled them.
- **Clearer choices**: scry and surveil say where the cards go ("Keep all on top", "1 card to the bottom, 1 on top"), and other card choices say how many you may choose ("Confirm 1 (up to 2)").
- **Card backs** across the table are seen upside down.
- **Fixes**: the token recruit creates has its picture; a land you play from another player's cards (exiled from their library with permission to play it) enters under your control.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Get the content module: download `arcanum-classic-v0.7.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest and, in Arcanum, open **Extras → Install module from zip…**. This version of the game needs module 0.7.0 or later.
3. The first start after updating downloads the card data again (a few minutes).
