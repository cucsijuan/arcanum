## What's new
- **More cards**: another Standard set and its commander set are fully playable, with its bonus sheet of reprints and its special guests, play and collector boosters and five welcome decks; career mode sells its boosters, five sample decks, two theme decks and five commander decks.
- **Increment and paradigm**: creatures that grow when you cast a spell with more mana than their power or toughness, and spells that, once resolved, let you cast a free copy at the start of your first main phase each turn for the rest of the game.
- **Spree**: choose one or more modes, paying each chosen mode's extra cost.
- **Roles**: Aura tokens that give a creature an ability; each player keeps only their newest Role on a creature.
- **Warp**: cast a creature cheaply from your hand; it's exiled at the next end step, and you can cast it from exile on a later turn.
- **Cases**: enchantments that become solved at your end step once their goal is met, unlocking their solved ability. The board announces it.
- **New keywords and abilities**: infect, casualty, echo, gravestorm, bestow, devoid, backup, strive, piles chosen by an opponent, Auras that bring a creature back from a graveyard, attack taxes per counter, and many more effects for commander games.
- **Rules**: prevention effects that deal the prevented damage back at once, a life total that damage can't drop below 1, spells that can't be countered for a turn, hexproof from colors, discards that may go on top of the library, and cards exiled from a hand until a creature leaves.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. This version needs the content module 0.25.0: install `arcanum-classic-v0.25.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…).
3. The first start after updating may take a moment to refresh the card data.
