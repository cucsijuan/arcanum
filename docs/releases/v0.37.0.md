## What's new
- **Regional championship online**: from December the regional championship can also be played online, against the other players checked in for the same start (Saturdays at 09:00 and 15:00 UTC; the computer takes the empty seats). It's the same event: your invitation counts, and you bring a registered deck of the event's format. Playing online pays more than playing it at the store, collector boosters included.
- **Two-day events online**: the first day's rounds end with the cut; the event is kept by the career service overnight and goes on the next day at the same time, in the same seat. If the host doesn't come back, someone else at the table takes over.
- **Sideboarding**: between the games of a constructed match, the deck changes only with its sideboard.
- **Round clock and tardiness**: online rounds are timed, with the end-of-match procedure when time is called. A player who isn't at the table when the match is due gets a game loss after a minute, and loses the match (and leaves the event) when still away ten minutes after it was due.
- **Prizes**: events can give boosters of other kinds (such as collector boosters) as prizes, and online events can have prizes of their own.
- **Tournament table**: the band across the mat no longer covers the tournament's crest.
- **Online events**: the check-in button shows up as soon as check-in opens, without leaving the screen.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. The content module stays 0.22.0. If you have it, no update is needed. Otherwise install `arcanum-classic-v0.22.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…).
3. The first start after updating may take a moment to refresh the card data.
