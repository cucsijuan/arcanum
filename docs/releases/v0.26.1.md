## What's new
- **Cancel**: when you cast a spell or activate an ability with a cost that sacrifices, discards, exiles cards from your graveyard, taps creatures or returns a permanent, you choose those cards before anything is paid, and you can cancel there.
- **Legendary permanents**: casting a legendary permanent you already control asks first whether to cast it anyway, since you would keep only one.
- **Damage on the board**: a permanent that deals damage with an ability (not in combat) now strikes at what it hits, like an attacker does. Damage from spells flashes on what it hits.
- **Announcements**: the board says when a triggered ability has no legal targets and is removed, and when a spell or ability does nothing because its targets are no longer legal.
- **Attached cards**: hovering a permanent whose attached cards sit under the phase bar brings them above it, so they can be seen and pointed at.
- **Token pictures**: tokens created later by a delayed ability ("at the beginning of the next upkeep") now show their picture. Tokens only take pictures of tokens with the same abilities, so a flying token no longer shows a token without flying.
- **The computer**: it no longer destroys its own permanents with optional removal. It casts X damage spells only when the X it can pay kills something. It no longer double-blocks a first striker that would kill the blocker that threatens it.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Content module: `arcanum-classic-v0.14.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…), if you don't have it yet.
