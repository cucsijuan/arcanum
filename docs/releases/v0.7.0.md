## What's new
- **Commander, four players**: the monarch (with its tag on the board), voting (open and secret council), control-changing effects applied in timestamp order, attack restrictions, mana in your commander's colors, can't-lose effects, and the commander companion set of the third card set with its four preconstructed decks (in the content module).
- **Many more mechanics**: multikicker, replicate, squad, storm, dash, delve, conspire, miracle, splice, split second, undaunted, exert, devour, persist and undying, dethrone, renown, exalted, skulk, regeneration, hideaway, suspend, cycling triggers, split cards with aftermath, snow mana, protection from players and more.
- **Online lobbies**: hosted games and draft/sealed events get an **invite code**; players on the same network see them in **Open lobbies** on the Online screen, or join with the code. Joining by address still works for everyone else.
- **Rules fixes**: the cleanup step repeats when something triggers during it, a player who leaves a multiplayer game takes their control effects and permanents with them, and effects on "creatures you control" never reach other players' creatures.
- Computer players use reanimation and sweepers better.

## Install
1. Download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Get the content module: download `arcanum-classic-v0.3.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest and, in Arcanum, open **Extras → Install module from zip…**. Module 0.3.0 needs this version of the game or later.
3. The first start after installing or updating the module downloads the card data (a few minutes).
