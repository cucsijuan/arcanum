## What's new
- **Ten picture playmats**: dragons of each color, a dark lord, dwarves, elves, humans and riders. Choose one in Settings like the other playmats; online opponents see it too. The career store sells them at 20.00.
- **Opponent's playmat**: it lies turned round, as across a table, in normal games and at the career table.
- **Picture playmats** fill their area without stretching, cut a little above the middle so figures keep their heads. Each playmat's cut can be tuned in the interface art (`kit/mats/mats.json`).
- **Career playmats**: regulars and visitors play on playmats that match how good they are. Newcomers and visitors often play without one; the best regulars play on the dearest.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Content module: `arcanum-classic-v0.14.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…), if you don't have it yet.
