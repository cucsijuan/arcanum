## What's new
- **Deck suggestions for every constructed format**: the deck builder's Suggestions now also work for casual, standard, pioneer, modern, legacy, vintage and pauper decks. They come from public community decks like yours (same colors, sharing your cards): each card shows how many of those decks play it, grouped into the cards not in your deck yet and by type. You can look at one of those decks or use the average of them.
- **Career deck desk**: casual decks get the same suggestions, marked as yours or not; each deck like yours shows how much of it you own (the ones you can build first), and using one builds it from your collection.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Content module: `arcanum-classic-v0.13.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…), for the deck suggestions.
