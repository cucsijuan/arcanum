## What's new
- **Another Standard set and its commander set**: every card plays by its rules, also the set's special guests, with play and collector boosters as released for draft and sealed, five two-color sample decks, the five commander precons in the career store and at commander night, and draft ratings for the computer drafters. It needs the content module 0.22.0.
- **New rules**: endure, mobilize, harmonize, omens, decayed, flanking, battle cry, mentor, riot, fabricate, eternalize and embalm, suspend, dredge, demonstrate, hexproof from monocolored, myriad from effects, experience counters (shown on the board), void counters, Gold tokens, curses that trigger when the enchanted player is attacked, creatures with defender that may attack players who attacked you, attacks forced on a random opponent, spending mana as though it were any color, "you may choose two" modes, damage divided among some targets while others are tapped, permanents that get the abilities of exiled lands, and many more effects for the new cards.
- **Board**: random and opponent choices made by the new cards are announced.
- **Fixes**:
  - "Attacks this combat if able" lasts only for that combat, not the whole turn.
  - A spell cast from the graveyard with "if it would be put into your graveyard, exile it instead" is exiled only then.
  - "Activate only if it has a +1/+1 counter" no longer counts other counters.
  - A spell that costs more for some targets offers only the targets it can still be paid with.

## Install
1. If you have 0.12.1 or later, the game offers this update when it starts. Otherwise download the zip for your system and extract it (keep the executable next to its `data_Arcanum_…` folder).
2. Update the content module too: `arcanum-classic-v0.22.0.zip` from https://github.com/cucsijuan/arcanum-classic/releases/latest (Extras → Install module from zip…).
3. The first start after updating may take a moment to refresh the card data.
