# Settings

Open **Settings** from the main menu. The screen has four tabs: **Gameplay**, **Appearance**, **Audio** and **Video**.
Switch tabs with the mouse, or with Q and E (or Page Up and Page Down). A short note explains each option.

## Gameplay

**Play**

- **Your name**: the name other players see online. It is also the name of the first seat on this device.
- **Confirm mana payment**: when on, you choose the lands to tap before a spell is cast. When off, the game pays for you.
  See [Playing](playing.md).
- **Show both hands in hotseat**: in a game on one device, every hand stays face up.
- **Animation speed**: how fast cards move, turn and fly across the table, from 0.5× to 2.5×. Above 1× is faster.
- **Full control**: stop at every point where you get priority, even with nothing to do.
- **Opening boosters**: how a booster opens in a draft: **Tear them open** (you swipe along its tear line), **Open
  automatically** (it opens by itself if you wait), or **No animation**. See [Limited](limited.md).

**Default stops**

A grid of the turn's steps, with a column for **Your turn** and one for **Opponents' turns**. Set the steps where the
game stops to let you act. You can change them during a game on the phase bar too. See [Playing](playing.md).

**Updates**

- **Check for updates at start**: look for a newer game, or a newer content module, when the game starts. If one is
  found, a dialog shows what is new, with **Update**, **Later** or **Skip this version**.

**Online**

- **Discord account**: **Sign in** or **Sign out** with Discord. The game only learns your Discord user id and name. See
  [Online](online.md).
- **Show activity on Discord**: while the Discord app is open on this computer, your Discord profile shows what you are
  doing in Arcanum: the screen you are on, or your game, its turn and your life total. It works without
  signing in. See [Online](online.md).
- **Time per decision**: for games you host, how long each player has for a decision: no limit, 30 seconds, 1 minute,
  2 minutes or 5 minutes. When the time is up, the computer makes the decision.

## Appearance

**Playmats**

The table under the cards, one for each side:

- **Your playmat** is the table under your cards.
- **Opponents' playmat** is the table under the computer's cards, and under an online opponent's cards when they use a
  custom image.

Each one is a choice from the built-in playmats, or **Custom image…**. The built-in playmats include a grid, plain
colored tables and picture playmats. Choose **Custom image…** to pick a picture from your computer. The file dialog
accepts PNG, JPG, JPEG and WebP files.

Online, your opponents see your playmat too, unless it is a custom image: a custom image is used only on this device.
The opponent's playmat lies turned round, as across a table.

**Card back**

The back of every hidden card: libraries, the hands of opponents and face-down cards. The choices are five built-in
designs (Arcane, Tide, Ember, Grove and Obsidian), and any designs the content module offers. A module design is
downloaded the first time it is shown. A preview shows the design.

**Foil**

- **Foil shine**: how strongly a foil card shines under the pointer, from 0% to 100%. Lower it if the shine makes the
  text hard to read. 0% turns it off.

## Audio

- **Master volume**: the level of everything the game plays.
- **Music**: the level of the music.
- **Effects**: the level of sound effects, such as the chime that starts your turn.

## Video

- **Fullscreen**: fills the whole screen, or plays in a window.
- **Window size**: the size of the window when not in fullscreen. Only sizes that fit your screen are offered.
- **V-Sync**: matches the screen's refresh rate. No tearing, a little more input delay.
- **Interface scale**: the size of everything on screen: text, buttons and cards, from 0.75× to 1.5×.

## Where settings are kept

Settings are kept on this computer. Your playmat and card back choices are part of them. Your career is kept separately.
See [Career](career.md).
