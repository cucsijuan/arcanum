# Limited: draft and sealed

Draft and sealed are limited events. You build a deck only from the cards you open in boosters, then play rounds
against the computer players at your table. Both appear in **Play** when the content module has cards.

- **Draft**: you open boosters with the other players and pass them around, taking one card at a time.
- **Sealed**: you open a set of boosters and build from everything you got.

## Start an event

1. From the main menu, choose **Play**.
2. Choose **Draft** or **Sealed** at the top of the mode list.
3. Choose the **Boosters** source. It lists the sets that have boosters, and any cube you added.
4. Choose the number of players: **Draft table** for a draft, **Players** for a sealed event. Choose 2 to 8.
5. Choose **Best of one** or **Best of three** for the matches.
6. Click **Start draft** or **Start sealed**.

### Add a cube

A cube is a list of cards you choose. Click **Add a cube…**, give it a name and paste the list, one card per line as
`1 Card Name`. A set code and collector number after a name choose its printing. Boosters of 15 cards are dealt from
the list.

## Draft

- The header shows the pack, the pick, and the direction the packs pass: **left**, then **right**, then **left**.
- Click a card to select it, then click **Take card**, or double-click it. The rest of the pack goes to the next
  player.
- Every player picks at the same time. The screen says **Waiting for the other players…** until they have picked too.
- Your picks are listed on the right, with a count.
- The computer players at your table draft as well.

In a career draft, each card also shows a grade and a win rate from the set's draft statistics, when the set has them.
See [Career](career.md).

## Sealed

Open your boosters the same way. Each set gives a number of boosters for a sealed event: six, unless the set says
otherwise. The cards you get form your pool.

## Build your deck

When the draft or the opening is over, you build a deck:

- Click a card of your pool to add it to the deck. Click a card in the deck list to take it out.
- The deck needs 40 cards or more.
- Basic lands are free. Add as many as you need.
- The pool is grouped by color.
- Click **Start playing** when the deck is ready. Online, the button reads **Deck done**.

## Play the rounds

- The event has three Swiss rounds, played against the computer players at your table.
- The match is best of one or best of three, as you chose.
- The screen shows the stage (drafting, building the deck or the round) and your record.
- When the event is over, the screen shows your place and your record. A win shows a celebration.

Undo is not available in an event. See [Playing](playing.md).

## Leave or abandon an event

If an event is running, **Play** shows **Draft in progress** (or **Sealed in progress**) with its source and buttons:

- **Continue** goes back to the event.
- **Abandon** ends it. The screen asks you to confirm first.

Online, the event screen has **Leave event** instead.

## Online events

You can host a draft or a sealed event for other players. See [Online](online.md).
