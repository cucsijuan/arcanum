# Career

Career is the single-player part of the game. You start with a deck, play matches against the store's regulars, earn
cash and cards, and build your collection. Everything happens on this computer, against the computer. It needs the
content module (see [Getting started](getting-started.md)).

## Start a career

Choose **Career** from the main menu. The first page asks you to **Join the store's league**. Choose the starter deck
you want to begin with, then confirm. A new member gets starting cash and some boosters of this week's featured set.
The boosters wait for you under **My cards**.

**End this career** (at the bottom of **My cards**) deletes your cards, your cash and your career decks for good. The save
is kept as a backup file in the career's folder.

## The room

The room is your home. Click the things in it, or move between them with the arrow keys and Enter. Press Esc to go back
to the main menu.

| Spot | What it opens |
|------|---------------|
| The computer | The store's website: daily matches, the forum, challenges and your cards |
| The desk | Your decks (see [The deck desk](#the-deck-desk)) |
| The stack on the desk | **My cards**: your collection, unopened boosters and **End this career** |
| The door | The store |

## The store's website

The computer opens the store's website over the room. Its pages are:

- **Daily matches**: today's match and your record.
- **Forum**: the store's events, match offers and buy and sell board.
- **Challenges**: this week's goals and what each pays.
- **My cards**: your collection.

## Daily matches

- You get two daily matches each day. The day starts at midnight UTC, so everyone has the same day.
- Each match is against one of the store's regulars. The page shows their style and the colors of their deck.
- Before the match, choose the prize for a win: **cash** or **boosters of the featured set**.
- A win pays more than a loss. A loss pays a smaller amount.
- Leaving a match before it ends counts as a loss.
- Daily matches you don't play are lost. They don't carry over.

## Match offers and the forum

The forum's **Match offers** board has more matches:

- **Practice** matches are unlimited. The first few each day pay a small amount. After that they give only reputation.
  Use them to test a deck.
- **Ante** matches are for a card. Each player puts a card up. You pick one of the cards the regular puts up, and offer
  one of yours. The winner keeps both. The board shows how many ante matches you can still play today.

The forum's **Events** board lists the store's announcements and today's events. See [Events](#events).

## Challenges

Each week has a few challenges, such as winning a number of matches or opening a number of boosters. The page shows
which ones you have done and what each pays. The list runs until the end of the week.

## Reputation and tiers

Matches, challenges and events raise your **reputation**. Reputation sets your **tier**. Each tier lets you meet tougher
regulars, and some events need a tier. Your tier never goes down. The tier names and the reputation each one needs come
from the content module. The **Challenges** page lists them and marks the one you have reached.

## Store

Walk through the door to the store. Its spots are:

- **Boosters**: the booster rack. It sells every kind of booster each set was sold in, with the price of each. Buy one,
  or open the ones you already have.
- **Preconstructed decks**: buy a ready deck. Its cards go into your collection, and the deck into your decks.
- **Sell cards** (the counter): sell cards to the store, at less than the store's price, or buy today's singles. The
  singles case changes every day and each single has its own price.
- **Today's offers**: the regulars' offers for the day. Each one is a **Buy** or a **Trade**: a card to buy, or a card you
  give for one you want. Once taken, an offer is marked **Taken**.
- **Playmats**: buy a playmat, use one you own, or take it off the table. See [Playmats](#playmats).

## Playmats

The store sells the game's built-in playmats, priced from 15.00 to 20.00 in the default content. Some of them are picture
playmats. Buying one puts it on the table at your matches and events. You can swap between the playmats you own, or
choose **No playmat**. A career starts without one.

Regulars and visitors also play on playmats. The better a regular is, the more often they use one, and the dearer it
is. Visitors and the lowest tier often play with none.

## My cards

**My cards** is your collection. It has:

- A filter for the cards you own.
- Your unopened boosters, with **Open one** for each kind. Opened cards go into your collection.
- **Sell extras**: sells every copy beyond the fourth of each card, or beyond what your decks use, after you confirm.
- **End this career** at the bottom.

Cards are marked when they are a foil, a special art, a collector art or a surge foil, as printed.

## The deck desk

The desk shows your decks. Choose a deck to edit it, or make a new one.

- **Owned cards** are on the left. They are grouped by name. Click a card to add a copy to the deck. Right-click a card in
  the deck to take a copy out.
- **The deck** is on the right, in columns by mana value (1 to 6+) and **Lands**. Basic lands are free.
- **The format** box sets the format the deck is saved for. The deck's problems are checked against that format, and
  the screen says **Ready to play.** when it is legal.
- **Commander**: in a Commander deck, right-click a legendary creature you own to make it the commander.
- **Suggestions**: a Commander deck with its commander, or a deck of another format, can show suggested cards from
  community decks. Cards you don't own are dimmed and can't be added. **Use average deck** builds the average deck from
  the cards you own.
- **Save** saves the deck. **Delete** deletes it.

You can only build decks from the cards you own. To get more cards, buy boosters, singles or preconstructed decks at the
store.

## Events

Events are on the store's events board, with the week's dates. Each one shows:

- The **format**: a draft of the featured set, a Commander pod (bring your Commander deck), or a constructed event
  (bring your deck).
- The players, the rounds and whether it is best of one or three.
- The **entry** (cash, boosters or qualification tokens) and the **prizes**.
- The reputation tier it needs, if any.

To enter, choose your deck if the event needs one, then click **Register** with the way you want to pay: cash, boosters
or a qualification token. The event screen opens. You can play an event once a day. Your qualification tokens are shown
on the page.

Some events are online at set times. The career screen says that **online play is coming soon**. Until then, you play
these events against the computer.

In an event:

- **Draft**: click a card on the table to take it. The rest of the booster goes to the next player. When the draft is
  over, build a deck of 40 or more on your desk.
- **Constructed**: play your rounds. Each round, play the next game of your match.
- **Commander**: a pod of four. Play the games for the night. **Go home early** ends the night for you.
- **Drop out** ends the event for you, in last place with your record so far.

When the event is over, the screen shows your place and your record, and your prize. **Collect and go home** adds the
prize to your career.

## Your save

Your career is saved on this computer. With a Discord sign-in, it is also kept in the cloud. Then it follows you to any
computer you sign in on. See [Settings](settings.md). If the cloud and this computer have gone apart, the game asks which
one to keep. The other is saved as a backup file.
